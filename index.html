THE PERSON BEHIND THE PIECES

Thoughtful art.
Distinctive stories.

I'm Mallory Cristian, an artist and illustrator drawn to expressive characters, meaningful details, and visuals that help a story stay with you. I work across sketches, line art, full-color illustration and book-cover concepts — adapting each piece to the idea it needs to bring to life.

Whether it's a character you've imagined for years or a story ready for its cover, I believe the best work starts with listening, exploring, and making something that feels like you.
